How prices are calculated

All sale counts include every row. Median prices include sales of $1,000 or more; lower prices are excluded because the data notes flag some as implausible. High prices are retained, and medians are used instead of averages.

What to keep in mind

Values are reported by the collection source, not verified against county records. Assessed value is not sale price. The dataset also includes a small number of extreme prices, and a town's median describes only the sales recorded here.

Source: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026.
